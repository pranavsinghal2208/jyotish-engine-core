    Sade Sati ?
    A 7.5-year Saturn transit over your Moon sign — a period of pressure, restructuring, and ultimately growth. Not a curse; a crucible. Three phases: approach, peak, departure. Each phase has distinct remedies.
    Mangal Dosha ?
    Mars placed in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12 creates extra intensity in relationships and drive. Not a curse — when channelled correctly, it is a force multiplier. Cancellations apply in many charts.
    Sarvashtakavarga — House Strength ?
    Your life is divided into 12 houses (career, relationships, health, wealth…). Each house gets a score 0–8 based on how many planets support it. 6+ = Strong · 4–5 = Moderate · 0–3 = Weak. Most people have only 2–3 strong houses — that's normal. Tap any house cell to see exactly what the score means for your life and what to do about it.

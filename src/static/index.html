    Dasha Timeline — Your Life Chapters
    Vedic astrology divides your life into planetary chapters called Maha-Dashas (major periods) — each lasting 6–20 years with a specific ruling planet and theme. Inside each chapter are sub-periods (Bhuktis/Antardashas) that tune the energy further. You are always in one chapter and one sub-chapter simultaneously. Tap a chapter to expand it.
    Active Yogas
    A Yoga is a rare planetary combination in your birth chart that gives you a specific edge — like a natural talent, a stroke of luck in a particular area, or a recurring life theme. These are yours for life.
    Sade Sati
    A 7.5-year Saturn transit over your Moon sign — a period of pressure, restructuring, and ultimately growth. Not a curse; a crucible.
    Mangal Dosha
    Mars placed in certain houses creates extra intensity in relationships and drive. When channelled, it's a force multiplier.
    Sarvashtakavarga — House Strength
    Your life is divided into 12 houses (career, relationships, health, wealth…). Each house gets a score 0–8 based on how many planets support it. 6+ = Strong · 4–5 = Moderate · 0–3 = Weak. Low scores are normal — most people have only 2–3 strong houses. Invest your energy in your strong ones.

    Varshaphal · Solar Return
    Every year on your birthday, the Sun returns to its exact birth position — resetting the annual clock. This chart shows the planetary setup for your current year and what theme it carries.
